FAQ

What you need to know.

The Fractals programs are intense Socratic inquiries. These answers clarify format, fit, care, investment, and what we will not promise.

What exactly are these programs?

They are structured Socratic intensives about how mind, identity, incentives, systems, ventures, and AI actually work. The emphasis changes by program: Radical Clarity focuses on inner architecture, Radical Agency on system architecture, and Radical Entrepreneurship on economic architecture.

Is this coaching, therapy, or a spiritual retreat?

No. The programs do not set goals, treat symptoms, resolve trauma, prescribe practices, or recruit you into a belief system. They are philosophical in method and practical in implication: rigorous inquiry into what can be observed and reasoned through.

Who is this really for?

Founders, CEOs, senior executives, investors, creators, builders, product leaders, technologists, system architects, solopreneurs, and other high-capacity humans with real leverage and real questions.

Who is this not for?

It is not a good fit if you mainly want tactics, frameworks, motivation, comfort, validation, tool demos, productivity systems, or clinical care. It is also not for people unwilling to question their own narratives and success stories.

How interactive is it?

The primary mode is dialog: questions, challenge, examination, live examples, and direct inquiry. Silent observation is allowed, but passive attendance is usually the wrong stance.

What about safety and confidentiality?

We insist on confidentiality and respect. There are no recordings by default. Safety here means the room is serious enough for honest inquiry, not that every moment will feel comfortable.

Where and when are programs held?

In-person programs are usually in San Mateo, CA or announced per cohort. We run a limited number of small cohorts so the work stays dialogic rather than becoming a conference.

What is the application process?

Submit the application form with your program interest, current context, and why this matters now. We review for fit, timing, and the right container before confirming next steps.

Do you promise outcomes?

No. We do not promise better leadership, better strategy, a specific valuation, emotional relief, or business results. We create conditions for clearer seeing. What you do with that clarity is your responsibility.
