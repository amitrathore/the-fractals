Radical Clarity

Consciousness in the age of AI.

A 48-hour Socratic intensive for high-capacity leaders who want to understand the architecture of their own mind and how it silently shapes organizations, relationships, capital, and AI systems.

Apply for Radical Clarity See the arc

The problem most high performers cannot name

You may be successful and still feel slightly off-axis, as if you are playing a game brilliantly without knowing who wrote the rules or whether you still believe in them.

Most leaders operate inside inherited scripts - identity, incentives, language, culture, expectations - without realizing those scripts are optional.

What Radical Clarity is

Radical Clarity is a precise, rigorous conversation that reveals how experience is constructed, how identity operates automatically, how language generates reality, how incentives shape life more than intentions, and how AI reflects your patterns back at you.

You do not learn new beliefs. You see the beliefs you already live inside.

Fit

Built for people who shape systems.

For

  • Founders and CEOs navigating complexity and responsibility.
  • Executives steering teams, culture, and high-leverage decisions.
  • Investors interpreting reality through models, incentives, and narratives.
  • Creators and builders who sense a deeper architecture behind their work.

Not for

  • People primarily seeking tactics, frameworks, motivation, or productivity systems.
  • People seeking therapy, clinical care, or emotional support.
  • People unwilling to question their own narratives, identities, and success stories.
Weekend structure

Eight domains of clarity.

The program consists of eight 90-minute sessions across a 48-hour weekend. The format is Socratic dialog: no slides, no jargon, no philosophical posturing.

01

Are You Awake?

Experience, perception, and map vs. territory.

02

Identity as Computation

How "you" runs automatically.

03

Language

The silent operating system that generates your Matrix.

04

Systems and Incentives

Actual constraints vs. assumed constraints.

05

Value and Exchange

Relationships as information systems shaped by fear, trust, and identity.

06

AI as Mirror

How AI reflects and scales your cognitive architecture.

07

Standing For Something

Action and commitment without ego-driven narrative.

08

The Fractal Human

Clarity without metaphysics, distortion, or dogma.

What you may encounter

Participants commonly report a quieter mind, a clearer view of identity, a cleaner relationship to fear, more honest incentives, and a simpler understanding of which games are worth playing.

These are not guaranteed outcomes. They are typical consequences of sustained inquiry.
