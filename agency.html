The age of agents is here

Power is shifting from static institutions to networks of human and machine agents coordinating across knowledge fabrics. Organizations become minds-of-minds, nested in larger org-of-org systems.

What Radical Agency is

Radical Agency extends the inner inquiry of Radical Clarity into the outer work of building agentic, human-centric systems. It connects consciousness, identity, semantics, values, power, protocols, ledgers, and games.

Design stance

Bridge inner architecture and outer architecture.

For

  • Leaders shaping AI-powered products, platforms, or organizations.
  • System architects designing agentic fabrics, knowledge graphs, and protocols.
  • Builders who want systems that are powerful, profitable, and genuinely human-centric.

Not for

  • A tactics course or AI tooling bootcamp.
  • A product strategy workshop.
  • An ethics checklist layered on top of unexamined power structures.
