A team of fractals

The minds behind The Fractals Company.

Amit Rathore founded The Fractals Company to make a long-running inquiry into reality, intelligence, systems, and AI directly useful to high-capacity humans.

Who is Amit Rathore?

Amit is a founder and systems thinker working at the frontier of consciousness, mind, and intelligence. His work asks how human and artificial intelligence experience, decide, coordinate, and create.

A sustained inquiry

Through iRealization, iConsciousness, and the Intergraph, Amit has been pursuing one question from multiple angles: how does intelligence construct reality and then scale that construction into systems?

The triangle

Three projects, one inquiry.

iRealization

An ongoing investigation of reality, the I, and the structure of direct experience.

iConsciousness

A study of artificial consciousness beyond merely scaling current AI models.

Intergraph

A knowledge and economic fabric for human and AI minds to coordinate, remember, and share value.

How Amit works in the room

  • Precise, but informal.
  • Deep, but grounded in direct experience.
  • Unimpressed by persona.
  • Non-ideological and unwilling to recruit you into a belief system.

How this fits The Fractals

The Fractals Company is the current vessel for work on the Fractal Mind, Radical Clarity, Radical Agency, Radical Entrepreneurship, and future work at the intersection of consciousness, AI, ventures, and global systems.
