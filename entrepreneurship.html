The problem most solopreneurs cannot name

Most entrepreneurship advice is still product plus funnel plus hope. But AI agents are changing the structure of work, and the next powerful ventures will look more like economic fields than solo products.

What Radical Entrepreneurship is

It is a rational inquiry into how community, incentives, AI agents, graphs, ledgers, and shared upside combine into ventures where a few hundred or a few thousand active participants can create meaningful value.

Canvas

The work is to make the game legible.

Community-powered venture canvas

  • Field and pattern: the world you serve and the recurring work/value loop.
  • Players and roles: contributors, curators, cosellers, operators, customers, partners, and agents.
  • Core game loop: how someone discovers, contributes, earns, and returns.
  • Economic flows: who creates value, who captures it, and which signals matter.

Agent-ready architecture

As agents become more capable, they will look for clear roles, clear rules, and clear flows. The program helps you identify where agents can coordinate, match, track, and support without swallowing the human game.
